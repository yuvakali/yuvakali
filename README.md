## 🌌   RESEARCH PILLARS

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🔭 Optical Systems
Multi-sensor optical payloads and lab-scale optical satellite testbeds for vision-based navigation and precision proximity ops.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### ☀️ Solar Panel Systems
Deployable solar array architectures and power-system integration for long-duration on-orbit servicing spacecraft.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🪂 Drag Sail
Passive de-orbit drag sail systems engineered for reliable end-of-life satellite disposal and debris mitigation.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🦾 Robotic Arm
Manipulator kinematics, capture-mechanism design, and closed-loop control for on-orbit grappling of debris and satellites.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🧠 GNC Algorithms
Guidance, Navigation & Control — EKF/MEKF attitude estimation, determination, and autonomous maneuver planning.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🌐 STOS
SpaceTug Orbital Simulator — full RPOD simulation platform with robotic-arm capture, physics core, backend, and live UI.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
